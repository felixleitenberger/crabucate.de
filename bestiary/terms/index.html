2. What the app does

Daily Bestiary offers one animal card a day. Which card that is, the app works out from the day you received your first card — not from a fixed calendar date. The day turns over at midnight in your device's time zone.

On first launch you receive three cards free of charge. They make the app complete in itself: front, back, facts, photo credit, collection and quiz. No purchase is required for any of that.

All content ships inside the app. Nothing is downloaded and nothing is generated.

4. Purchases

There are three kinds of purchase:

  • Card of the day — unlocks today's card. Repeatable, once a day.
  • Ten-card pack — credit for ten unlocks. The credit does not expire and is spent on the card of the day.
  • Collector — a subscription. It additionally opens the archive, where missed days can be claimed, includes the card of the day, and shows quiz statistics across the whole collection.

Your contract for a purchase is with Apple, not with the developer. Billing runs through your Apple Account and Apple's App Store terms apply.

You can cancel at any time through Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period; until then your access remains. If you cancel during a free trial, no payment is due provided you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

The developer cannot cancel a subscription on your behalf; Apple grants that option only to you.
