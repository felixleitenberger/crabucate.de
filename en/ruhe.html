Help & Support

Ruhe doesn’t measure anything – what now?

Ruhe needs microphone access. Allow it in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Microphone → Ruhe.

The light turns red too early or too late.

Tap the slider icon in the toolbar and move the yellow and red thresholds – or use “Set Automatically” while the class works normally.

How do I show Ruhe on a projector?

Connect your iPad to the projector by cable or AirPlay. Ruhe automatically shows the noise light full screen there and keeps the controls on the iPad.

How do I reset the flames?

Tap the flame counter at the top left. You can adjust the flames there or reset them to 0. If Ruhe was in the background for more than 10 minutes, the next lesson starts at 0 anyway.

Is anything recorded?

No. Ruhe only measures the volume – nothing is recorded, stored or sent. Details are in the privacy policy.
