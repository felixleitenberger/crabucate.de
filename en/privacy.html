Privacy Policy

Team Zufall · Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Team Zufall does not collect any personal data, does not send information to external servers, and uses no tracking or analytics. All data (e.g. groups and participant names) is stored exclusively on your device and never leaves it.

What data is stored?

The app stores your groups and participant names locally on your device — exclusively so you can find them again when you next open the app. This data is not transmitted to us or any third party.

In-app purchases

Team Zufall offers optional in-app purchases. All payments are processed exclusively through Apple and the App Store. We have no access to your payment data. Apple's Privacy Policy applies.

Third parties

The app does not include any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics services.

Children

The app is not specifically directed at children under 13. No personal data from children is collected.

Changes

If something fundamental changes that affects this policy, we will update it. The date above indicates when the policy was last changed.
