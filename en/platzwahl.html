Platzwahl

The classroom seating chart app for teachers: create a seating chart in one tap — automatic, fair, print-ready as PDF.

Free·iPhone & iPad·No account·Student data stays on your device

Features

Seating plan at the tap of a button

One tap — Platzwahl distributes all students automatically and in accordance with the rules you've set. No manual rearranging, no trial and error.

Customise your classroom

Place desks, leave gaps, position the teacher's desk — recreate your classroom exactly as it is. Any layout is possible.

Rules per student

Disruptive tendencies, seating preferences, sit at the front — individual rules feed directly into the calculation.

Score & violations

Every rule violation is explained and weighted. You can see exactly why a plan is good — or still needs improvement.

Export as PDF

Clean seating plan as PDF — print it, place it on the desk, done. No post-processing needed.

Platzwahl is for teachers who need a working seating plan in the morning — not an hour to create one. Set up the room once, add your class, define rules. From then on, the app does the work: it distributes everyone fairly, displays rule conflicts transparently, and delivers the result as a print-ready PDF. No cloud, no sign-in, no distractions.

How it works

  1. Rebuild your classroom

    Draw your room as a grid — tap seats or paint whole areas with your finger. Rows, U-shape, table groups: any layout works.

  2. Add your class & set rules

    Enter students or import them via CSV from Excel. Then mark per student: disruption level, front-row seat, preferred neighbors, separations.

  3. Tap "Optimize"

    The genetic algorithm computes the best possible seating chart. A score shows how well all rules are met — every deviation is explained.

  4. Print as PDF

    Export the finished chart, print it, put it on your desk. Need a new one? One tap.

FAQ

Who is Platzwahl for?

For teachers of all school types who want to create seating charts quickly and fairly — without spending hours manually rearranging or fiddling with spreadsheets.

How does Platzwahl automatically create a seating chart?

The app distributes students based on individual rules — disruptive behaviour, seating preferences, gender separation — optimally across the available seats. A score shows at a glance how good the chart is and which rules are still violated.

Are student data stored or shared?

No. All entered names and rules remain exclusively on your device. There is no transmission to external servers — neither by us nor by any third party.
