Platzwahl

Seating plans that actually work — automatically created, fairly distributed, print-ready.

Features

Seating plan at the tap of a button

One tap — Platzwahl distributes all students automatically and in accordance with the rules you've set. No manual rearranging, no trial and error.

Customise your classroom

Place desks, leave gaps, position the teacher's desk — recreate your classroom exactly as it is. Any layout is possible.

Rules per student

Disruptive tendencies, seating preferences, sit at the front — individual rules feed directly into the calculation.

Score & violations

Every rule violation is explained and weighted. You can see exactly why a plan is good — or still needs improvement.

Export as PDF

Clean seating plan as PDF — print it, place it on the desk, done. No post-processing needed.

Platzwahl is for teachers who need a working seating plan in the morning — not an hour to create one. Set up the room once, add your class, define rules. From then on, the app does the work: it distributes everyone fairly, displays rule conflicts transparently, and delivers the result as a print-ready PDF. No cloud, no sign-in, no distractions.

FAQ

Who is Platzwahl for?

For teachers of all school types who want to create seating plans quickly and fairly — without spending hours manually rearranging or fiddling with spreadsheets.

How does Platzwahl automatically create a seating plan?

The app distributes students based on individual rules — disruptive behaviour, seating preferences, gender separation — optimally across the available seats. A score shows at a glance how good the plan is and which rules are still violated.

Are student data stored or shared?

No. All entered names and rules remain exclusively on your device. There is no transmission to external servers — neither by us nor by any third party.

Can I manage multiple classes and rooms?

Yes. You can create as many rooms and classes as you like, each with their own seating plans. Everything is saved.

Is there a PDF export?

Yes. Every seating plan can be exported directly as a PDF — clean, effortlessly, immediately ready to print.
