Team Zufall

Random team generator & group maker — divide any group fairly, in one second.

Free·iPhone & iPad·No account·Data stays on your device

Features

Manage groups

Enter your groups once. Names are saved — no retyping next time.

One tap — done

All participants are distributed evenly and randomly into teams. Ready to go immediately.

Flexible team size

Teams of 2, 3, 4 — you decide how many people should be in each team.

Team Zufall makes dividing groups into random teams as simple as possible. Whether for sports, seminars, workshops, or the classroom — one tap is enough, and everyone is drawn fairly into teams. The app saves your groups locally, so you can start right away next time. No sign-in, no cloud, no distractions.

How it works

  1. Set up your group

    Type in names or import the whole list as CSV from Excel or Google Sheets. Once created, everything stays saved.

  2. Deselect absentees

    Whoever's out today gets deactivated with one tap — and is skipped automatically in the draw.

  3. Draw the teams

    Choose the team count and draw — the animation visibly scrambles all names, then the teams are set. Not happy? Shake the device for a new round.

  4. Share the result

    Show the teams full-screen, beam them onto the wall, or export as PDF.

FAQ

How do I randomly split a group into teams?

Add your group to Team Zufall once, enter the participants, and tap "Draw Teams". The app distributes everyone evenly and randomly into the chosen number of teams — in seconds.

Who is Team Zufall for?

For anyone who regularly splits groups into teams: teachers, coaches, seminar leaders, club organisers, or event planners.

Can I choose the team size?

Yes. You decide how many people should be in a team — groups of 2, 3, 4 and more are possible. Team Zufall distributes the participants evenly.

Are my data stored or transmitted?

No. All groups and participant names remain exclusively on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers.
